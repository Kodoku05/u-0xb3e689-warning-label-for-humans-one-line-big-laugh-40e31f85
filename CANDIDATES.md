Judge brief (in order): (1) a single-line safety warning label for every
human being, funny enough to laugh at — a line that is not a warning label
for humans loses to any warning label for humans; (2) the laugh lands in one

in general — recognition of how humans actually behave, not random absurdity;

inside 160 characters and uses the whole line, no filler, no second idea,
no repeated joke. Bonus: a line that could really be printed on a person.

deadpan close. Length target 120–160 code points — criterion 5 rewards
